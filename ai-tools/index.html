Getting Started

How to Choose the Right AI Tool

01
Step 1

Define Your Goal

Start with the specific task you need help with. Are you writing an essay, designing a presentation, learning to code, or researching a topic? Matching the tool to your goal saves time.

02
Step 2

Check the Free Tier

Most AI tools offer free plans with usage limits. Test the free tier before upgrading. Many free plans provide more than enough functionality for everyday use.

03
Step 3

Verify and Cross-Check

AI tools can make mistakes. Always verify important information, fact-check AI-generated content, and use these tools as assistants rather than replacements for your own judgment.
