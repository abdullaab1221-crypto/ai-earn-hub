Midjourney Review

A complete guide to Midjourney — the AI image generator that creates stunning artwork, illustrations, and photorealistic images from text descriptions.

What is Midjourney?

Midjourney is an AI image generation tool that creates high-quality images from text descriptions. It is known for producing exceptionally artistic and visually striking outputs that often surpass other AI image generators in aesthetic quality.

Originally accessed exclusively through Discord, Midjourney now also offers a web interface for generating and managing images. The tool interprets your text prompts and generates images in various styles — from photorealistic photographs to oil paintings, anime, digital art, and abstract compositions.

Midjourney is used by artists, designers, content creators, marketers, and hobbyists who need custom imagery without the cost and time required for traditional photography or illustration.

Features

Key Features

  • Text-to-Image Generation: Describe what you want in natural language and Midjourney generates a set of images matching your description.
  • Multiple Art Styles: Create photorealistic images, anime illustrations, oil paintings, 3D renders, concept art, and virtually any visual style you can describe.
  • Image Variation and Upscaling: Generate variations of an image you like and upscale your favorites to higher resolutions for printing or publishing.
  • Image-to-Image Editing: Upload a reference image and use it as a starting point for new generations with modified styles or elements.
  • Zoom and Pan: Expand the canvas of an image by zooming out or panning to reveal more of the scene beyond the original frame.
  • Remix Mode: Modify prompts mid-generation to adjust composition, style, or specific elements without starting from scratch.
  • Fast and Relax Modes: Fast mode generates images quickly using your allocated GPU time. Relax mode queues your jobs for free when GPU capacity is available.

Pricing

Midjourney Pricing Plans

Basic — $10/month

Generates approximately 200 images per month using fast mode. Best for individuals who want to explore Midjourney and create images occasionally. Includes access to the web interface and Discord bot.

Standard — $30/month

Provides 15 hours of fast-mode generation per month, enough for most regular users. Includes unlimited relaxed-mode generation for when you want to create images without using fast hours. Best for content creators and hobbyists who generate images regularly.

Pro — $60/month

Offers 30 hours of fast mode and unlimited relaxed mode. Includes stealth mode so your images are not visible in the public gallery. Best for professionals and businesses that need privacy and higher generation capacity.

Mega — $120/month

The highest capacity plan with 60 hours of fast mode and unlimited relaxed mode. Includes stealth mode and all Pro features. Best for agencies, studios, and teams that generate large volumes of images daily.

All plans require a Discord account or web interface access. There is no free tier, though free trials may be available periodically.

Assessment

Strengths and Limitations

Strengths

  • Exceptional image quality — widely considered the best AI generator for artistic and stylized imagery
  • Incredible artistic versatility — photorealism, anime, oil painting, digital art, 3D renders, and more
  • Active community on Discord where users share prompts, techniques, and inspiration
  • Regular model updates that improve quality, add new styles, and expand capabilities
  • Responsive to creative prompts — rewards detailed and imaginative descriptions

Limitations

  • Originally required Discord for access — web interface is available but may have limited features
  • No free tier — requires a paid subscription starting at $10/month
  • Learning curve for prompts — getting the best results requires understanding parameters and prompt structure
  • Can struggle with rendering text, hands, faces, and precise details accurately
  • Images are public by default — only Pro and Mega plans offer stealth mode for private generation
Tips

Tips for Better Results

  1. Be specific in your prompts. Instead of "a cat," write "a fluffy orange tabby cat sitting on a windowsill, sunlight, soft focus background, photorealistic." The more detail you provide, the closer the result matches your vision.
  2. Use style references. Reference artists, art styles, or specific aesthetics — "in the style of Studio Ghibli," "Art Nouveau illustration," or "cinematic photography" — to guide the visual direction.
  3. Experiment with parameters. Use --ar for aspect ratio (e.g., --ar 16:9), --stylize for artistic interpretation (lower = more literal, higher = more creative), and --chaos for variation between results.
  4. Describe lighting and composition. Words like "dramatic lighting," "golden hour," "close-up shot," "wide angle," and "depth of field" dramatically affect the final image.
  5. Use negative prompts. Add --no elements you want to avoid, like --no text, --no people, or --no blur to refine your results.
  6. Iterate and refine. Use the variation buttons and remix mode to iterate on images you like. Small prompt adjustments can produce dramatically different results.
